02 / 06 Compact Numbered Chips

Smaller cards, number-forward design, densely packed for maximum slide visibility.

Trades thumbnail detail for density: 96 px wide chips show more slides without scrolling. The oversized number becomes the primary navigation cue; an accent underbar replaces the full ring.

  • 96 px wide · 16:9 · no visible border by default; 3 px accent bottom bar + shadow on active
  • Large bold number top-left (15 px, accent colour when active) — number is the UI, thumbnail is context
  • Compact 4 px gap fits ~8 slides in view; slide title label below each card at 9.5 px
  • Ghost Duplicate / Delete buttons on hover · narrower "+" Add affordance · collapse at far right
