03 / 06 Elevated Carousel

Active card lifts up and scales larger; neighbours dim and recede — spatial focus through elevation.

A tactile, carousel-inspired rhythm: the current slide floats above the rail while non-adjacent cards fade into the periphery. Orientation is instant — you always know where you are.

  • Active card: scale(1.12) + translateY(–8 px) + overlay shadow — unmistakable focal point
  • Adjacent cards (2, 4): scale(0.98) · opacity 0.88 — softly recede but remain readable
  • All other cards: scale(0.96) · opacity 0.75 — context without distraction
  • Rail has 20 px top padding to give the lift room; overflow-y: visible ensures nothing clips
