05 · Segmented Header Tabs

Replaces the underline tab strip with a pill-track segmented control and adds a persistent section subnav for fast jumping within the panel.

  • Segmented control uses var(--ds-segment-track) / var(--ds-segment-thumb) tokens
  • Section subnav (TMPL · GEO · FILL · TYPE · EFX · LYR) lets users jump to anchors
  • Tabs and subnav are both sticky — always visible while scrolling content
  • Raised thumb shadow distinguishes active segment from track without color
