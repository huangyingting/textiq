01 · Minimal Statusline

A quiet, information-dense single line that recedes into the chrome. Controls are invisible until hovered, keeping visual noise near zero while all status remains accessible.

  • Hairline 1px separators (var(--ds-border-subtle)) between groups
  • No backgrounds on buttons — color shift on hover reveals affordance
  • Uniform 11px muted text; active states use secondary/primary
  • 5 px status dot carries save signal without text-label weight
