02 · Pill Zoom Control

Key controls are rendered as small tactile pill-shaped widgets, giving the bar a "control-forward" feel. The connected zoom pill (−/value/+) and colored save pill are the primary focal points.

  • Connected three-segment zoom pill — zoom out | value ▾ | zoom in
  • Save status is a colored pill (green/amber/red) with dot prefix
  • Toggle buttons (Slides, Notes) use pill silhouette with sunken fill
  • Presence avatars overlap with a 2px white ring for depth
