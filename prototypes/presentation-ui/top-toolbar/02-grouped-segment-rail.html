02 — Grouped Segment Rail

Each logical group lives inside a recessed pill-shaped track, like a row of connected keycaps sitting in a groove. Controls snap within their rail rather than floating free.

  • Groups wrapped in --ds-surface-sunken rails with inset shadow
  • Active/hover controls lift as a raised "thumb" using --ds-segment-thumb + --ds-shadow-raised
  • Solid white toolbar background makes sunken tracks visually pop
  • Rail dividers (1 px) separate sub-groups within the same conceptual area
