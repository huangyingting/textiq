05 · Adaptive Two-Row

Primary row shows the highest-frequency controls (B/I/U/S, role, size, align, color). A chevron button at the far right expands a secondary sunken-background drawer for advanced controls (lists, indent, link, overflow). Minimal by default; powerful when needed.

  • Row 1: highest-frequency controls always visible
  • Row 2: advanced controls in sunken drawer (surface-sunken)
  • Chevron rotates 180° when expanded — clear affordance
  • Smooth max-height transition on expand/collapse
  • 3 selection modes: TEXT / SHAPE / SLIDE
