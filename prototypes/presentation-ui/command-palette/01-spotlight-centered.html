01 — Spotlight Centered

macOS Spotlight–inspired floating panel: a full-width search bar backed by icon-box rows. Generous spacing and the accent-filled left border make the active selection unmistakable at a glance.

  • 560 px centered panel with --ds-surface-overlay base and --ds-shadow-overlay.
  • 32×32 icon boxes: sunken bg at rest, --ds-accent-surface + accent border when active.
  • Active row: --ds-state-selected fill + 3 px --ds-accent left bar; enter chip turns accent-filled.
  • Row shows label + muted section sub-label; shortcuts appear as --ds-font-mono kbd chips on right.
  • Empty state with muted icon + copy; footer legend anchored to sunken strip.
