01 — Floating Bottom Bar

A compact pill-shaped HUD centred 20 px above the stage floor — stays weightless above the slide, fades into the dark stage on idle, never obscures content.

  • Single pill surface with --ds-inverse-surface + 24 px backdrop-blur — recedes on the dark stage without competing with slide content
  • Nav arrows get a raised --ds-inverse-control chip to signal primary navigation action; icon-only utility tools stay ghost
  • Timer is self-contained in a bordered chip for instant scannability at a glance during the talk
  • Thin 2 px accent progress bar runs the full width at the absolute stage floor — minimal but always present
  • Exit adopts a warm red hover tint to signal destructive intent without polluting the resting state
