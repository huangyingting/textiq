05 — Edge Docked Rail

A 48 px wide vertical icon strip pinned to the right stage edge — zero horizontal footprint, maximum slide real estate, with a vertical progress bar running along its inner border.

  • All presenter controls live in a 48 px rail using --ds-stage-panel so it reads as a native part of the dark stage rather than an overlay
  • Tooltips slide in from the left on :hover — labels are always present in the DOM but visually hidden until needed
  • Nav arrows sit at the top; timer and slide counter anchor the bottom — mirrors a physical teleprompter layout
  • A 2 px vertical progress bar on the inner edge of the rail fills from the bottom up, matching a physical "how far along" metaphor
  • Rail takes zero horizontal slide space; the slide mock fills the rest of the stage edge-to-edge beside it
