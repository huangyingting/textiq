01 — Flat Outline

Hairline 1px borders, flat fills, no shadows. The quietest, most precise control language — every edge is drawn, nothing is implied by depth. Best for dense editorial toolbars where information density matters most.

  • 1px solid borders, var(--ds-border-subtle) at rest → var(--ds-border-strong) on hover
  • Active state: var(--ds-accent-surface) fill + var(--ds-accent-border) ring + var(--ds-accent-text)
  • Zero elevation — var(--ds-shadow-flat) everywhere
  • Focus: 2px solid var(--ds-accent) offset 2px
  • Height 28px (sm) / 32px (md), radius var(--ds-radius-md)
