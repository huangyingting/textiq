02 — Soft Raised

Every control sits on a white raised card with a gentle drop shadow. Depth is implied through light rather than borders — surfaces feel like physical keys with tactile, clicky hover feedback.

  • All surfaces: var(--ds-surface-raised) + var(--ds-shadow-raised) at rest
  • Hover: shadow deepens (0 2px 6px), surface stays white
  • Active: accent fill + colored shadow glow; icon buttons go fully filled
  • Segmented track: inset shadow (sunken) + raised white thumb on selection
  • Switch: inset shadow off, accent glow on
