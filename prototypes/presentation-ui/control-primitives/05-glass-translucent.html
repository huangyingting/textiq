05 — Glass Translucent

Every surface is a frosted glass pane — translucent white fills with backdrop-filter: blur. Controls float above the page, revealing blurred content underneath. The language is layered, luminous, and modern.

  • All surfaces: rgba(255,255,255,0.45–0.88) + backdrop-filter: blur(10–18px)
  • Borders: rgba(255,255,255,0.5–0.8) — bright hairlines catch the light
  • Inset top highlight: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.8–1) simulates glass edge
  • Active: accent tint overlay instead of solid fill; maintains translucency
  • Backdrop: subtle color gradients visible through the glass surfaces
